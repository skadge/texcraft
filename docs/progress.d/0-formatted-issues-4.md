- 2026-11-26: The formatted view after testing (#44–#48). **Front matter** (#44): elsarticle's `frontmatter` is a title block (`BlockKind::FrontMatter`): the title, each author with superscript affiliation numbers and note signs (`*` corresponding, `†` title, `‡` author notes) and their `\ead` in typewriter, the affiliations (elsarticle's `organization={…}, city={…}` as its values), the notes, then the abstract and the `keyword` environment (a "Keywords" block, `\sep` shown as ·); the title, names, e-mails, affiliations and notes are edited as text, the marks are the view's (`Style::sup`). The parser gives `\ead`, `\affiliation`, `\address`, `\tnotetext`, `\cortext`, `\fntext` and `\tnoteref`, `\corref`, `\fnref` their arguments. **Three periods** (#45) typed in text become `\dots`, an edit of its own after the typing so Undo brings the periods back; not in math, verbatim, typewriter text or non-text arguments; a letter or space typed right after a control word (`\dots`, `\LaTeX`) gets `{}` before it so it does not lengthen the name or vanish. No setting yet (Settings is being changed elsewhere). **Empty items** (#46): an empty paragraph is laid out a line tall (an empty section with the text's format), so the caret of an empty list item stands in its line instead of half a line above. **List buttons on an empty line** (#47): `edit::set_list` makes a list with one empty item and the caret in it there (returns a `SourceEdit`). **Deleting list items** (#48): `edit::deletion` / `Doc::deleted` remove the items a selection across lines holds whole with their `\item` lines (the environment when it holds them all) and join two partly selected items of one list; typing or pasting over such a selection deletes first, then types. Tests: latex (front matter of an invented elsarticle fixture, `crates/latex/tests/fixtures/frontmatter.tex`; ellipsis contexts; deletions of whole, partial, nested and two lists; a list on an empty line; hostile sweep with deletions) and kittests (title block shown and edited, `...` and Undo, deleting and typing over items, the list button on an empty line, an empty item's line height). Not yet: accented names (`\'e`) show as source in the title block; several `\author`s with `\maketitle` outside `frontmatter` show only the last.
