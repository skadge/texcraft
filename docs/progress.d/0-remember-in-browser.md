- 2026-11-28: The browser keeps sign-ins and tokens in its local storage for the site, so every tab is signed in and stays so across restarts and days (Settings › Accounts › Remember sign-ins in this browser, on by default, also beside Sign In with GitLab; off keeps them in the tab only, for a shared computer, and the other tabs forget them; `accounts.remember_in_browser`). A sign-in, a renewal, a Disconnect or the setting changed in one tab shows in the others without a reload (`accounts.reload` on the browser's `storage` event); an operation reads the sign-in again before renewing it and after a refused renewal, so two tabs do not spend GitLab's one-time refresh token twice. Accounts says "In this browser" or "In this tab".
