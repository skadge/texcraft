- AI1 and AI2 (docs/ROADMAP.md): AI assistance, off until the user connects a model, on the desktop. Built on the suite's AI layer at the pinned craft-libs rev: `craft-ai-core`'s `ConnectionDraft` checks the connection and derives from the address whether data leaves the computer (a loopback address is local and needs neither token nor consent), `Disclosure` and `ConsentLedger` decide when to ask, `HistoryRecord` is the request log; `craft-ai`'s `OpenAiCompat` talks to the endpoint; the token goes to the system's keychain through `craft-suite-account`'s `Keychain` (the suite's one keychain namespace), never into the preferences, which keep the connection (with a reference to its token), the model and the agreements. Settings › AI: the endpoint, the token (kept in the keychain; the box empties once connected), Connect, Test Connection, Forget (the connection, its token and its agreements), the model picked from the endpoint's list (List Models), "Use AI in this project" (kept in the project's `.texcraft/ai.json`) and the request log (when, how many bytes, to where, which model, how it ended; never the text). Errors and warnings on a build log card, and build messages in the problem popup, have an Explain button: the first explanation for a connection opens a dialog showing exactly what is sent (the instructions, the message and its help, the source lines around it, and the main document's preamble when the message is in it) and to which address, with Agree and Explain or Don't Send; the explanation then shows in the card (and the popup) with a spinner and Cancel while the model answers, and errors in words. A fix the model gives (the lines it was sent, corrected, between markers) becomes a suggested change by "AI (model)" covering only what differs, never an edit, and is not offered when those lines changed while it answered. Commands for agents: `ai.status`, `ai.connect {endpoint, token?, model?}`, `ai.model`, `ai.models {background?}`, `ai.forget`, `ai.project {enabled}`, `ai.disclose {index | path, line}`, `ai.consent {request: "explain"}`, `ai.explain {index | path, line, background?}`, `ai.cancel`; each refuses plainly without a connection, a model, consent, or in a project that turned AI off. Tests (a fake model behind `craft-ai`'s provider trait; nothing reaches a network): refusals without a connection, nothing sent before consent and consent asked again for a new address, the text sent equal to the disclosed text and nothing further from the file or other files, the preamble only for a message in it, a fix becoming a suggestion and not an edit (accepting it makes exactly the fix), no fix on lines changed meanwhile, a local endpoint needing no token or consent, the token in the secret store and not in the saved settings and gone after Forget, the project's opt-out kept in its folder, a cancelled answer dropped (engine); Settings › AI connecting and listing models with no token in the preferences, and Explain declined then agreed with the answer in the card (kittest). Not yet: the browser (`craft-ai`'s `OpenAiCompat` is desktop-only, so Settings › AI says AI is not available there), streaming the answer, and cancelling stops waiting but cannot stop a request already sent (the suite's provider call is blocking). New dependencies, all MIT or Apache-2.0 (sysinfo MIT; byteorder Unlicense OR MIT; objc2-io-kit Zlib OR Apache-2.0 OR MIT): craft-ai, craft-ai-core, craft-suite-account and what they bring (keyring, secret-service, sysinfo).
