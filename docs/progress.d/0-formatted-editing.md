- 2026-11-26: F3 (docs/ROADMAP.md): the formatted view becomes a way to write. **Its toolbar** (`formatted_bar`) replaces the stale "Read-only for now" hint: a paragraph-style drop-down (Body Text, Heading 1–3 as the document's sectioning levels, `\chapter`/`\section`/`\subsection` for book, report, memoir and the KOMA and ctex book classes, else `\section`/`\subsection`/`\subsubsection`; `texcraft_latex::edit::heading_commands`), Bold, Italic, Code (`\texttt`), Bulleted and Numbered List, Outdent and Indent Item, Link, Footnote, Citation, Cross-Reference, a Formula menu (inline, displayed, numbered) and Table and Figure, each its `edit.*` command with its shortcut in the tooltip, groups giving way to a More menu as the source toolbar's do. The buttons show where the caret is (`edit::here`: Bold pressed in `\textbf`, the paragraph style, the list kind) as toggled buttons and a combo box named "Heading 1, paragraph style". New commands: `edit.body` (Cmd+Alt+0), `edit.heading_1..3` (Cmd+Alt+1..3), `edit.chapter`, `edit.code` (Cmd+Alt+C), `edit.footnote {text}` (Cmd+Alt+N), `edit.indent` (Cmd+]) and `edit.outdent` (Cmd+[); `edit.link {url, text?}` (Cmd+K), `edit.cite {key}` (Cmd+Alt+B), `edit.ref {key}` (Cmd+Alt+R), the formula commands `{latex}` and `edit.figure {image}` take their parameters from agents, and in the formatted view without them ask: a picker (`insert_pick`) of the project's `.bib` entries (read with retex-bib, CC0, shown as "Knuth (1984), The TeXbook") or labels (with the heading, caption or environment each labels, `texcraft_latex::refs`), or of its images for a figure, a typed key allowed; a one-line field for a link's address, a footnote's text or a formula. Citations and references go in after a word with `~`, `\cref` where cleveref is loaded; a link wraps a selection in one run; a table goes after the block holding the caret, ruled, with a header row, its first cell selected. In source mode the new commands do the same at the caret (headings by class, body, indent, outdent; with parameters, the insertions). **Typing across structure** (`edit`): Enter on an empty item ends the list (in a nested list it goes one level out); Tab and Shift+Tab in a list item nest it under the item before (joining the list that item ends with) and take it one level out (at the top, a paragraph between the list's two parts), Tab still leaving the view outside lists and cells; Backspace at a heading's start makes it body text, at an item's start lifts the item; pasted lines starting with `-`, `*`, `•`, or `1.`/`1)` become lists; undo by word was already the source editor's (a word goes with the space typed after it). **Spelling in the view:** right-click or Shift+F10 opens the text's menu, on a misspelled word its spellings, Add to Dictionary and Ignore first, then Cut, Copy, Paste and Select All; a spelling replaces the word's source alone; `edit.spelling.*` act on the view's caret when it is shown (`spell::keep`, a thin helper shared with the source). **Cross-links:** a citation's tooltip gives each key's author and year; Ctrl+click on a reference goes to its label, in this file or another. Fixed on the way: a paragraph made a heading or a list took the line end before it inside the command (`\section{\nIntroduction}`); a caret just inside `\textbf{` was shown after the first letter, so a second style wrapped the word from its second letter.
