- 2026-11-25: AI4 and AI5 (docs/ROADMAP.md): making LaTeX, drafting references, asking about the project and reviewing a section with the connected model, on the desktop. Insert › Make with AI… (`edit.ai_make`) asks for a description of a table, an equation, a TikZ diagram or a figure environment for one of the project's images; it sends the description, the main document's preamble (so the model uses the packages it loads) and, for a figure, the names of the project's images. The model's LaTeX is typeset after the preamble before it is offered: a scratch document built once by retex in a copy of the project (`texcraft_compile::scratch`, one folder per run), its first page drawn in the dialog cropped to its ink; when it does not typeset the dialog shows retex's messages (their lines counted in the LaTeX given) and offers Ask Again with the Error, which sends the last answer and the messages back, or Insert Anyway. Packages the answer needs that the preamble lacks (a `PACKAGES` block, or the preamble of a whole document the model wrote anyway) are typeset with it and, on Insert, become a second suggestion before `\begin{document}`. Insert makes the LaTeX a suggested change at the caret by "AI (model)", on lines of its own, never an edit. Insert › Add a Reference with AI… (`edit.ai_reference`) sends only the title or DOI typed; the dialog says first that models invent references and that the entry is a draft to check, never a verified record; the entry (checked to be one `@type{key, …}` with balanced braces, its key changed when the `.bib` already uses it) becomes a suggested change at the end of the chosen `.bib` (made when it does not exist) under a line `% Drafted by AI (model): check it`. The rail's Ask AI section (`view.side.ask`) sends a question with the open file, or by the user's choice every text file of the project (`.tex`, `.bib`, `.sty`, `.cls` and the like, at most 150,000 characters), with numbered lines; the answers are kept in the panel, newest first, and the `path:line` places they name that exist become links that open the file at the line. Review This Section (in the panel and Edit › Review This Section with AI, `edit.ai_review`) sends the section at the caret (from its heading to the next of its level or above, or the document's body when there is no heading; at most 16,000 characters); each remark quotes the words it is about and becomes a comment by "AI (model)" on the first place those words are (spacing aside) at or after the section's start; remarks whose quote is not in the text are dropped and the status line says how many. Each is its own kind of request (`text.make`, `text.reference`, `text.ask`, `text.review`): the first one for a connection opens the consent dialog showing exactly what is sent, and the Make and Reference dialogs come back after it; agents cannot agree for the user. Commands for agents: `ai.make {kind, description, path, at, again?, insert?, background?}` (by default inserts when it typesets), `ai.insert_made {anyway?}`, `ai.reference {query, bib?, background?}`, `ai.ask {question, scope?, path?, background?}` (its `links` with character offsets in the answer), `ai.review {path, start, end, background?}` (its `comments` and `dropped`), `ai.disclose` and `ai.consent` with `request` make, reference, ask or review.
