- AI3 (docs/ROADMAP.md): rewriting a selection and continuing a paragraph with the connected model, on the desktop. Rewrite with AI, in the Edit menu (and the system menu bar on macOS) and in the source's context menu when text is selected: Clearer, Shorter, More Formal, Fix Grammar and Spelling, and Translate to… with fourteen languages. A rewrite sends the selection and up to 300 characters of whole lines on each side; it is its own kind of request (`text.rewrite`), so the first one for a connection opens the consent dialog showing exactly what is sent (agreeing to explanations does not cover it). The answer becomes a suggested change by "AI (model)" on exactly the selection, never an edit, keeping the selection's own leading and trailing spaces; it is refused, with a message naming what changed, when it dropped or altered a `\label`, a `\ref`-family key, a citation key or a formula (compared whole, spacing aside), or dropped a command, and when the selection changed while the model answered. The check is `texcraft_latex::keep::compare`, a scanner over the passage (comments skipped, escaped dollars kept as text). Settings › AI › "Suggest the next words as I type" (off by default; turning it on asks consent for `text.complete`, showing an example of what is sent): after a one-second pause in typing at the end of a line, with the caret where the typing left it (Vim: in Insert mode), the paragraph up to the caret (back to a blank line or `\begin{document}`, at most 1500 characters, never in the preamble) is sent, at most once every two seconds; the model's next words (one line, at most 160 characters) are drawn in grey after the caret and announced in the status line; Tab takes them (in Vim's Insert mode too), Escape or typing on drops them, and an answer for text that changed meanwhile is not shown. Another request (an explanation, a rewrite) takes over from a continuation in flight; cancelling a continuation leaves other requests alone. Commands for agents: `ai.rewrite {path, start, end, how, language?, background?}`, `ai.complete {path, at, background?}`, `ai.disclose {request: "rewrite" | "complete", …}`, `ai.consent {request: "rewrite" | "complete"}`, `ai.cancel {only: "completion"}`; an interface command `edit.rewrite {how, language?}` runs the menus' entries. Tests: the LaTeX check accepting a reworded passage and naming a dropped `\ref`, altered formulas, labels, citations and dropped commands, comments and escaped dollars, half-written answers (latex); a rewrite refused without its own consent and nothing sent, the text sent equal to its disclosure and nothing further from the file, the rewrite a suggestion and not an edit with accepting it replacing exactly the selection, a rewrite that drops a `\ref` refused saying so, a continuation refused without consent, rate-limited, of the paragraph up to the caret only, and not shown when typed over, and cancelling a continuation leaving an explanation in flight (engine, against a fake model); Rewrite with AI › Shorter from the context menu asking first and making a suggestion card, and the next words appearing after a pause and taken with Tab, with and without Vim mode (kittest). Not yet: the browser (no provider for a web page in the suite's AI layer), the next words in the formatted view, streaming.
