- 2026-11-22: Edits, not texts. The editor sends each change to the session as an edit (`file.edit {path, edits:[{start, end, text}]}`, character offsets, with its origin: typing, undo, redo, a peer, a review, or outside TeXCraft), and the session keeps a log of them that the editor and review anchors follow; `file.write` and Replace All record the smallest edits that make their text. Undo skips a peer's or an outside edit: it takes back only one's own change, and the other's text stays.
