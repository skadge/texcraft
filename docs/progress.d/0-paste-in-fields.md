- 2026-11-25: copy and paste in every text field, and the primary selection. The desktop app was built without eframe's `clipboard` feature, so egui-winit kept a clipboard of its own inside the window: Ctrl+V and middle click in Settings' fields (the AI endpoint and token among them), dialogs, the find bar and every other field could not paste what another program had copied, and nothing copied in TeXCraft reached other programs. With it on, the fields paste, copy and cut through the system clipboard (a password field pastes; it still does not copy), and on X11 and Wayland selecting text sets the primary selection and a middle click pastes it where it lands. The source editor and the formatted view, drawn by TeXCraft, offer their selection as the primary one once it settles, and a middle click on the source text pastes the primary selection at that place (not under a dialog over it). The Edit menu's Cut, Copy, Paste, Select All, Undo and Redo chosen with the pointer act on the field that had the keyboard, with its selection, instead of the editor; Cut and Copy are no longer disabled there for want of a selection in the editor. The keyboard paths were already right: the keymap leaves the clipboard keys and the text-editing chords to a focused field. New crates, all MIT, MIT OR Apache-2.0 or Zlib: arboard, smithay-clipboard, wl-clipboard-rs and their dependencies. Tests (kittest): a paste, Ctrl+A, Ctrl+C, Ctrl+X, Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Shift+Z in a dialog's field and a Settings field leave the editor alone; the editor still pastes, and Vim's Normal mode keeps Ctrl+V as its own key; a middle click pastes at its place in the editor and only in a dialog's field over it; selecting in the editor, a field and the formatted view sets the primary selection; the Edit menu acts on the find bar's field, then on the editor once it has the keyboard again.
