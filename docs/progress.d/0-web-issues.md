- 2026-11-25: the web editor on a phone (#12) and SVG text in the browser (#5). Below the theme's `narrow_width` (700 points, on the desktop too) the window is laid out for a phone: the side panel starts hidden and the rail opens it as a drawer over the panes, which closes on Escape, on a tap beside it and once a file or a place in one is chosen (the editor then shows it and takes the keyboard); the keyboard goes into the drawer when it opens and back to the rail when it closes. The editor and the PDF are two tabs, Source and PDF (`view.pane.source`, `view.pane.pdf`; Left and Right move between them; the PDF's tab shows and names the build's state: compiling, errors, warnings, compiled); a line to go to shows the Source tab, Show in PDF the PDF's. The menus are one Menu holding File, Edit, Insert and Help, the project's name sits beside it, cut short when long, and the layout switch gives way to the tabs; the PDF's pages and zoom are a second toolbar row. Widening the window brings back the side panel and the layout it had. `ui.inspect` reports `narrow` and `pane`. The garbled text on the phone came from egui's glyph atlas being as wide as the largest texture (8192 px under WebGL 2): read at half precision, as phones' GPUs can, glyphs past its middle were sampled four texels at a time (the screenshots show blocks of four identical columns); the browser build caps textures at 2048 px. SVG figures draw their text with the interface's faces as well as the system's (in the browser the only ones), the generic families going to faces there are, and a family missing from both standing for the generic one its name suggests (sans-serif for Roboto) instead of usvg's serif fallback. Tests: kittests at phone size (tabs and their keys, the drawer opening, closing and moving the keyboard, choosing a file, widening, the top bar), and SVG text drawn with only the host's fonts.
