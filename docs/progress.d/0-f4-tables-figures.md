- F4 (docs/ROADMAP.md): tables and figures in the formatted view. `texcraft_latex::table` reads a `tabular` as a grid: its column specification (`l c r`, `p{…}`/`m{…}`/`b{…}` and `|`), rows split on `\\` (with its `*` and `[…]`) and `\tabularnewline`, cells on `&`, both at the body's top level so a group or an argument stays in its cell, `\multicolumn` spans, and `\hline`, `\toprule`, `\midrule`, `\bottomrule`, `\cline` and `\cmidrule` between rows; what it cannot show faithfully it names (`\multirow`, a table or another environment in a cell, `@{…}`, `!{…}`, `>{…}`, `<{…}`, `*{…}` and unknown column types, a rule inside a row, text beside a `\multicolumn`, more cells than columns, `\noalign` and kin, a table not closed), and such a table stays as its source with the reason in its header and accessible name. Its edits change only what they must: a cell's text; a row above or below (a row under a header's rule goes under the rule; a last row without `\\` gets one); a row deleted with its line; a column left or right, or deleted, in the specification (with its `|` where the table has them) and every row, a `\multicolumn` across it growing or shrinking by its count; a column's alignment by its letter alone; borders none, all (`|` everywhere and `\hline` between all rows, rows inserted and deleted keeping one rule each) or booktabs (`\toprule`, `\midrule` under the first row, `\bottomrule`, partial rules renamed between `\cline` and `\cmidrule`), each rule renamed, added on a line of its own or removed with its line. booktabs is never added to the preamble: the edit returns the package and the window says when the main document does not load it. The view shows a `tabular`, alone or in a `table` float holding it with `\centering`, a caption and a label, as a grid: columns as wide as their widest cell (scaled to the measure), each cell's text set by its column's alignment (or its `\multicolumn`'s) and edited in place as a line of the view's text (typing escaped, so `&` is `\&`), the table's own rules and bars drawn, faint lines for every cell while the caret is in it, and the caption with its label above or below as in the source. While the caret is in a table a floating toolbar over it offers Row above, Row below, Delete row, Column left, Column right, Delete column, the column's alignment and the borders, each a `table.edit` command. Keys: Tab and Shift+Tab select the next and previous cell's text, Up and Down go to the cell above and below (and out of the table from its first or last row), Escape goes to the toolbar and Escape there back to the cell; dragging a selection now tells side-by-side cells apart. Accessibility: the grid is a table of rows of cells, each cell with its row and column index (and span), the toolbar a toolbar named Table. Figure cards have an Edit Figure… button opening a dialog: the image (from the project's images, written as LaTeX finds them from the main document), its width as a share of the line (a width in other units is kept unless the slider moves), where LaTeX may place it (here, top, bottom, a page of floats; `!` and `H` kept), the caption as LaTeX source and the label; Apply runs `figure.edit` with the parts that changed, each changing only its own source or added on a line of its own. Commands for agents: `table.inspect {path, at?}` (each table's columns, rows of cells with their text and span, borders, or why it stays as source), `table.edit {path, at, op, row?, column?, text?, latex?, align?, borders?}`, `figure.edit {path, at, image?, width?, placement?, caption?, label?}`, `figure.insert {path, at, image, …}` (a new figure on lines of its own, a project image written from the main document's folder). Tests: reading, refusals, every edit and the border round trip, a hostile-input sweep over mutated tables and all edits (latex); the figure edits; the commands and the booktabs note (engine); a cell edited in place changing only that cell, Tab to the next cell, the toolbar's Row below, a refused table's reason and the figure dialog's caption (kittest). Not yet: merging and splitting cells from the toolbar, the caption's position, `tabularx`/`longtable`/`tabular*`, Enter in a cell (refused, as in a caption), a cell's formulas and fallbacks edited in place, and in the browser an image dropped on the view is only added to the project. On the desktop an image file dropped while the formatted view is shown is added to the project and put in a new figure after the block holding the caret (`figure.insert`, label `fig:<name>`, caption left for the dialog); this path has no automated test (kittest cannot drop OS files).
