Formula is one drop-down (inline, displayed, numbered, and Symbols…) and References another (Cross-Reference, Citation). Where their labels would push the first groups out, the Suggest switch shows its pencil alone and the Source/Formatted switch two icons, each keeping its name and showing it on hover (`widgets::segmented_with`). Citation and Cross-Reference in the source open the formatted view's pickers (the project's `.bib` entries, its labels with their caption or heading) and insert `\cite{…}` or `\ref{…}` (`\cref` with cleveref) after the selection, tied to the word before it with `~` as the formatted view does; Escape gives the keyboard back to the source. **The whole document's outline**: `texcraft_latex::document::outline` follows the main file's `\input`s and `\include`s (on a line of their own, as the numbering does) and gives each file's headings in their place with the numbers the PDF gives them, and an entry for an include that is missing, that would read a file already being read (a loop) or that is too deep; the session keeps it per revision (`Session::document_outline`) and agents get it with `file.document_outline`. The Outline shows it as one tree when the shown file is part of the document (else the shown file's own, as before): the shown file's headings in the text's colour with the heading icons, the other files' muted with a file icon and, where a file's part starts, its name; each row tells the screen reader which file it is in and whether that is the one shown. A click or Enter (the arrows move through the rows) opens the entry's file at its line; an include that is not read is said in words in its place.
