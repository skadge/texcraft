- 2026-11-26: F5 (docs/ROADMAP.md, UX review finding 5): the formatted view for readers who do not know LaTeX. **Numbers from the build**: a build hands back its `.aux` files (`Outcome::aux`, from retex's written outputs; the browser's worker sends them with the PDF), which `texcraft_latex::auxfile` reads (`\newlabel` with cleveref's type or hyperref's anchor, `\bibcite` plain or natbib's); `Aux::reference` says what `\ref`, `\eqref`, `\pageref`, `\nameref`, `\autoref`, `\cref` and `\Cref` show (hyperref's and cleveref's English names, cleveref's plurals for a run of one kind), `Aux::citation` what the `\cite` family shows (plain "[1, 2]", natbib's author-year "[Knuth, 1984]" and "Knuth [1984]" or its numbers, as retex's PDF sets them), or why not (`Missing`: not built, no such key, biblatex, `\nocite`). **The document counted** (`texcraft_latex::numbers`): from the main file through its `\input`s and `\include`s in place, headings to `secnumdepth` (letters after `\appendix`), the numbered math environments row by row (`\nonumber`, `\notag`, `\tag`), theorem-like environments by `\newtheorem` (shared counters, `[section]`), figure and table captions, counters reset by the chapter in a class with chapters or as `\numberwithin` says; a labelled part takes the build's number and sets its counter. The session keeps the parsed `.aux` and the numbers (`Session::aux`, `numbered`, `shown`, `included`; `language.numbers` reports a file's references and numbered parts for agents). **The view**: a resolved reference or citation is a quiet pill with what the PDF shows (still one unit when editing; the key, the author-year and Ctrl+click on hover), an unresolved one its key with "?" in a grey pill and why on hover; labels are an icon, their key on hover; headings carry their number, a numbered formula its numbers at the right of each row in place of its environment's name, figure and table captions "Figure 2." and "Table 1."; theorem-like environments (new `BlockKind::Theorem`, their optional argument read even when the parser does not know the environment) show "Theorem 2 (Title)." over their body as formatted text, a proof "Proof." and ∎. An `\input` or `\include` on a line of its own (new `BlockKind::Include`) shows the file's text in place between thin rules under its name, read-only, with Edit Here (opening it in its own formatted view, as a double-click or Enter in it does), the files it includes nested, a file that includes itself or is missing said in words; the included files' formulas are typeset in the same batch (`Session::formulas_of`). Choice: read-only in place rather than edited there, since the caret, undo and in-place editors all work on the shown file's buffer; a second buffer per included file is the next step. Tests: the `.aux` of a real retex build (sections, an `align` with `\nonumber`, an unlabelled equation, a figure, a theorem and a lemma sharing its counter, an included file's equation, a citation with a small `.bib`) mapped to what the view shows, before and after the build (engine); natbib's and cleveref's forms, theorem blocks and included files in the view model (latex); chips before and after a build, the numbered heading, formula and theorem, included files nested and missing, Edit Here (kittest). Not yet: names in the document's language (babel), biblatex's labels, `\cref` ranges, page numbers, theorem bodies in italics, editing an included file in place.
