- E9: folding and several carets. `texcraft_latex::fold::regions` finds what folds: environments spanning lines (math and verbatim ones too; not `document`), sections up to the next heading of the same or a higher level or `\end{document}` (trailing blank lines left out), and the preamble from `\documentclass` to before `\begin{document}`; one region per first line, the widest. The editor's gutter has a fold marker on each region's first line (shown when folded, on the caret line, or with the pointer over the gutter); a folded region keeps its first line with a "…" chip and its other lines take no room, while line numbers keep counting them. Both the marker and the chip are buttons named "Fold lines 12–40, section Introduction" (or Unfold), the marker with its expanded state; a fold or unfold is said on the status line, which is announced. Folds are kept per open file as buffer marks on their first line, so they follow edits; a fold unfolds when its region is gone (its structure broke) and when the caret lands inside (Go to Line, search, a diagnostic, a jump from the PDF or the outline, a motion). Left and Right step over a fold, Up and Down skip it. Commands: Edit › Fold (`edit.fold`, Cmd+Shift+[), Unfold (`edit.unfold`, Cmd+Shift+]), Fold All (`edit.fold_all`, Cmd+Alt+Shift+[), Unfold All (`edit.unfold_all`, Cmd+Alt+Shift+]); in Vim `zc`, `zo`, `za`, `zM`, `zR`, and `j`/`k` step over a fold rather than open it. Several carets, outside Vim mode: Alt+click adds a caret (or takes one away; Ctrl+click stays Go to Definition), Cmd+D selects the word at the caret, then adds each next occurrence; typing, Backspace, Delete, Enter, Tab, the arrows, Home and End act at every caret, each edit at all of them one undo step; Copy and Cut take every selection, one per line; Escape returns to one caret; the number of carets is announced. Limits: other commands (Comment, Tab with a selection over lines, completion, closing brackets) act on the primary caret only; a run of typing at several carets is undone a character at a time; Vim's `dd` on a folded line deletes only that line (the fold then opens); folds are not kept across sessions.
