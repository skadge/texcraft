- C3 (R2 of docs/collaboration-roadmap.md): suggested changes. A suggestion is a review thread with a `suggestion` (`texcraft-review`: author, time, `replacement`, `outdated`): its anchor is the passage it replaces (empty for an insertion), and it follows edits as a comment's anchor does; an edit inside the passage makes it outdated (rejectable, never applied). Kept as files of kind `suggestion` in `.texcraft/review/` (docs/review-format.md) or in the browser's library, like comments. Commands: `review.suggest {path, start, end, text, id?}` (with `id`, changes one's own), `review.accept`, `review.reject`, `review.accept_all {path?, author?}`, `review.reject_all`, `review.list {kind}`; accepting is one edit with the review origin, Accept All one per file, and the first of overlapping suggestions is taken while the others are reported outdated. An undo of an accept (an edit of origin `undo` that puts the passage back) reopens the suggestion, a redo accepts it again. Suggest mode (the toolbar's Suggest switch, Edit › Suggest Changes, Cmd+Shift+E, `:set suggest` in Vim) shows the file with its suggestions in it, each passage struck through and its replacement underlined in the author's colour, and reads edits of that text as suggestions (`texcraft_review::view`): typing on extends one's own, deleting marks text and moves the caret past it, an edit inside someone else's is refused with a message. Cards in the margin and the Review panel say the change in words ("Replace “colour” with “color”") and as struck and underlined text, with Accept, Reject, replies, Withdraw, and an outdated note; Accept All and Reject All act on the file shown. Builds ignore suggestions; `build.suggestions {on}` ("Show suggestions in the PDF", in Suggest mode's line and the Review panel) builds with them applied without writing them, and the line says which text the PDF shows. Limits: outside Suggest mode a replacement is only in its card (the passage is struck through, an insertion point marked); an accept made in Suggest mode is not in the editor's undo history; in Suggest mode completion, hover and go to definition are off and diagnostics keep the file's places; suggestions outdated by an accept stay outdated when it is undone; an insertion is found again on load by its offset only (context matching comes with R4); with suggestions in the PDF, SyncTeX's lines are those of the text with them.
