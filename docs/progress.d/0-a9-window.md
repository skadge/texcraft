- A9 (docs/ROADMAP.md), more of the window, three of its four parts. **The menus from the keyboard:** `view.menu_bar` (F10, or Alt pressed and released alone with no key, click or other modifier between; not on a Mac) puts the keyboard on the File menu's button; Left and Right move between the menus and wrap, Down, Enter or Space open one with its first entry focused (egui's menus then take Up and Down, and Left back to the menu's button), Escape closes the open menu and then gives the keyboard back to where it was, as choosing a command does. The menu bar's buttons draw the focus ring. **Focus mode:** `view.focus_mode` (Cmd+Shift+Enter) hides the menu bar, the rail and the side panel, keeping the editor and, in the split layout, the PDF; the status bar keeps a Leave Focus Mode button with the shortcut beside it; Escape leaves when nothing had the keyboard (so the Escape that leaves the editor, or Vim's own, does not); F10 or Alt shows the menu bar while the keyboard is in it. Per session, not kept in the preferences; `ui.inspect` reports `focus_mode`. **The PDF in its own window** (desktop): `view.pdf_window`, and a toggle in the PDF pane's toolbar (a new icon, `assets/icons/window-out.svg`, drawn for TeXCraft), moves the PDF pane into a second OS window (an egui immediate viewport titled "<project>: PDF"), whose keys run the same shortcuts; the main window shows the editor alone. Closing that window, the toggle again, or choosing a layout puts the PDF back. Show in PDF brings the PDF's window forward, and a click in the PDF to its source the main window. In the browser the command is disabled with the reason and the toggle is not shown; `engine.commands` now gives the interface commands' real `enabled` and `disabledReason`, and `ui.inspect` reports `pdf_window`. F6 stays within the main window. Tests (kittest): F10 and the arrows, Enter, Escape back to the text; Alt alone and not Alt with a key; focus mode's hidden parts, F10 in it and the two Escapes; the PDF window's toggle widening the editor and a layout bringing the PDF back (the harness shows the second window inside the first). Not done: **the macOS menu bar** through craft-menu (present at the pinned craft-libs rev, with `mac::install`, `NativeMenu::raw_input`, `take_clicks` and `sync`), because this machine has no macOS target or SDK to even type-check the AppKit glue; and the in-window menu bar is TeXCraft's own over egui's `MenuBar` rather than craft-menu's `bar` (which has Alt, F10 and mnemonics already), a move worth its own change.
