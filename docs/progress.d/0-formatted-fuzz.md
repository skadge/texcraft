- 2026-11-27: Random-edit runs of the formatted view, and what they found (docs/testing.md, "Testing the formatted view"). **The model** (`crates/latex/tests/it/formatted_fuzz.rs`): documents of the corpus (the templates, the examples, the crate's fixtures, three documents written for it; `TEXCRAFT_FUZZ_CORPUS` adds a folder of one's own papers, read and never copied) or slices of them, edited by random sequences of clicks, selections, arrows, typing (LaTeX's special characters, other scripts, a no-break space, a combining accent), Backspace and Delete, Enter, Tab and Shift+Tab, pastes of lines and lists, the toolbar's buttons and the table toolbar's operations, made as the window makes them; after each step: no panic, sound changes near the selection, nothing balanced made unbalanced, no empty item, list, style or heading left where the caret is not, nothing turned into source, the caret where the view shows it, and the view showing what the edit meant; a failure is shrunk (nodes, lines, characters of the document; steps) and printed. **The window** (`crates/ui-egui/tests/it/formatted_fuzz.rs`): random keys, text, clicks, drags, double and triple clicks, toolbar buttons, undo and redo on the real view with the build's fonts; after each action: no panic, the frames settle, the keyboard stays in the text, the caret is in a line inside the window, a typed letter shows before it without its line jumping, undo then redo give the texts back. Both run a short default (`TEXCRAFT_FUZZ_CASES`, `TEXCRAFT_FUZZ_WINDOW_CASES`, `TEXCRAFT_FUZZ_SEED` for long runs). **Fixed**: a space typed at a paragraph's or an item's end showed the caret at the next paragraph's start (`Doc::locate` now takes the text and keeps a caret past only spaces or an empty style's opening on its line, and prefers a line with a stop at the byte, so a table's caption line above with its label below no longer takes the caret of every cell); typing `~`, `^` or `\` showed the caret in the next paragraph and Backspace left `{}` (a symbol command's empty group belongs to its character); a paragraph made a list or a heading with the caret at its end put the caret after `\end{…}` or `}` (`edit::map_inside`), as did a pasted list (`edit::paste_caret`), Tab on a new empty last item and Backspace on an empty item between two (`indent`, `outdent`); a paragraph starting with `\textbf{…}` or `{…}` made a heading or a list was wrapped from inside the group (a paragraph's range covers its commands and groups whole); text pasted or typed over a selection right after a control word, or a word unbolded after `\item`, or `\dots` before a word, lengthened the command's name (`\itemplain`; `edit::joined`, and `edit::kept_apart` on every edit of the view); a deletion holding a style's or a heading's whole text left `\textbf{}` or `\section{}` (they go too, unless the deletion starts inside them, for the text typed next); whole items whose text ends in a styled word were not taken whole; Enter at a styled run's start, or on an empty style, Backspace and Delete in an empty style, left empty styles; text typed over whole items went before the next `\item` (the list became source; `edit::into_item`); text typed over a selection starting in an empty heading went after it, and a paste read its context at the caret rather than where it goes (a list pasted into a heading); an empty heading's caret stood before its command (typing went in the paragraph before; `view::heading_slot`), and typing after a heading's label made a paragraph (`edit::typing_point`: it extends the heading); clicking at a paragraph's start drew an empty line above it, Enter at the start of a container's, an item's or an included file's first paragraph and at the end of a document's last paragraph (before `\end{document}`) showed no empty line, nor did a paragraph left empty next to a list or a heading, or Bold pressed in an empty line (`edit::empty_paragraph_at`, shared by the window and the tests); a theorem or quote emptied, a title, author or date emptied had no line for the caret (`view::body_start`, the title block's `slots`); a float's caption line with no caption was a caret place where typing broke the float (it is not, and a table's empty `\caption{}` is a place to type its caption, as a figure's is); a table or an equation inserted with the caret in the title went into the preamble; merging cells left the caret before `\multicolumn`, so typing turned the table into source (`table::cell_caret`); a typed no-break space showed as a space or vanished. **Not fixed**: in a run of dashes or quotes the view shows as one character (`--` typed inside `---`), the caret shows at the run's start (the harness allows it); Backspace in the middle of a logo (`LaTeX`) does nothing; a heading made inside a list item stays one.
